🏛 INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Six Scholarly Research Pillars & Wings

Structured to surpass ordinary Islamic portals by operating as a full-scale academy of textual verification, codicology, and contemporary intellectual engagement.

01 • دار التخريج والعلل

Unit of Takhrij & Hidden Defects ('Ilal)

Tracing every Prophetic narration across primary Musnads, Sunan, and Ajza' to detect subtle narrator slips (Wahm), Tadlis, and hidden breaks.

02 • خزانة المخطوطات

Center for Rare Islamic Manuscripts

Digitizing, collating, and critically editing unprinted Hadith and Fiqh manuscripts with full verification of Sama'at (audition registers).

03 • موسوعة رجال الإسناد

Biographical Atlas of Narrators (Rijal)

Mapping teacher-student networks, geographical migrations, and chronological layers (Tabaqat) of classical transmitters.

04 • فقه السنة ومقاصدها

Department of Fiqh al-Sunnah & Synthesis

Harmonizing seemingly conflicting narrations (Mukhtalif al-Hadith) and deriving balanced legal rulings for contemporary global realities.

05 • مركز الرد على الشبهات

Epistemology & Defense of Prophetic Sunnah

Publishing peer-reviewed responses to historical skepticism, Orientalist critiques, and modern anti-Hadith fallacies in English, Urdu, and Arabic.

06 • دار النشر والترجمة

Abu Muhammad Khuram Shehzad Academic Press

Publishing the collected works of Abu Muhammad Khuram Shehzad alongside critical research journals and open-access primers.
